The community findings behind [Guide 06: Characters, Mounts and Scene Props](../06-characters-mounts-and-scene-props.md), as mined from the Discord export, with a jump link to the message for each one.

- **Access:** links open in Discord and only work for members of the Mount & Blade Modding server. Deleted messages and departed users won't resolve.
- **Where links land:** each link points to the best-matching message by the credited author on that date. That is usually the statement itself, sometimes the question or reply next to it, so read the surrounding thread.
- **Paraphrase:** claim text is paraphrased. The credited people are the source; the wording is ours.
