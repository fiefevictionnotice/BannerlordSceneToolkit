### WEAPONS (#bl-3d-art)

- **Weapons share a texture atlas** : vanilla weapon parts (blade, pommel, guard, grip) share UV sets, and several weapons sit in one texture. Pack your parts the same way. Engine batches the parts into one mesh on CPU and instances it.<br>TH3WICK3D1 2020-04-21/27, gkx 2020-04-24 · confirmed (dev) · [gkx, 2020-04-24](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/703169375389351936)
- **Sword import chain** : export blade/guard/grip/pommel as separate meshes. Add each as a `CraftingPiece` (id, piece_type, mesh, length, weight, culture), then a `CraftedItem` with `crafting_template` (e.g. OneHandedSword). Non-craftable items still use pieces. Entries go in the module XML (spitems etc.).<br>svelok/Dawa, 2020-10-17 · confirmed (several used) · [svelok, 2020-10-17](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/766998978729607209)
- **Part orientation** : model the sword blade pointing along +Z, with the grip point at the origin, then position other parts as if the sword were whole. Pieces overlap in Blender and line up in game if centred on origin. XML length/offset must match.<br>PlebeianKilo, AverageAlien, svelok, 2020-10-12 to 10-14 · single-report · [PlebeianKilo [ER], 2020-10-13](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/765790979399614486)
- **Skinning on a weapon material hides metal** : a musket with skinning enabled had invisible metal parts, and crafting pieces vanished in hand until some material settings were disabled. Skinning is for armour only.<br>PlebeianKilo 2020-10-13, 에른스트 2020-10-14 · confirmed (two reports) · [PlebeianKilo [ER], 2020-10-15](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/766404477287661578)
