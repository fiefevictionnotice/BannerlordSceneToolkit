### NAVMESH (#bl-scenes)

- **Auto-generate navmesh takes 1-8+ hours, needs lots of RAM and gives poor results; Generate Grids ignores terrain/trees and crashes for some. Hand navmesh for a big scene ~1 day. Set navmesh for every level/agent type, not only civilian L1. A "save navmesh only" option exists for quick saves.**<br>NIN3, Lemmy1916, Suspicious Man, Cuce, 2020-10-03..10 · confirmed · [NIN3, 2020-10-03](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/761302555308720148/761935980760006676)

- **AI off-navmesh just turns toward the target and runs, sticking on everything. Limit movement with limited navmesh or invisible barrier entities; make river faces more expensive so AI avoid them. Weld pieces and grow one navmesh outward; path barrier stair sides; "Show agent capsule debug" helps (crashed once).**<br>NIN3, Kipsta, shackers, 2020-10-06/08 · confirmed · [Kipsta, 2020-10-06](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/761302555308720148/763003982984773642)
- **How auto-generate works and its limits: voxelizes the terrain physics, filters walkable spots by agent radius, builds crude triangles and subdivides where the ground is too far. TW says navmesh is not automated yet; big faces hurt pathing and a patch with automatic divisions was planned.**<br>Muret (TW), 2020-10-10/12 · confirmed · [Muret, 2020-10-12](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/761302555308720148/765093209512345620)
- **Navmesh rule: no face may be farther than 1.5 m from the physics surface the agent steps on. "Mark Elevation Problem Faces" (navmesh editing mode) logs offenders, double-click log entry to jump to them; "select inverted faces" in Debugging Tools finds flipped faces.** Grid-generated meshes ignore props/trees; delete grid faces under walls or AI think they can walk through. Generate Grids at size 3-5 m with "Use Boundary" ticked and a border_soft entity works for terrain-only battle maps (too-large grids do not hug terrain; spill past borders into outer mesh).<br>Muret (TW), FoozleMcDoozle, Suspicious Man, 2020-10-10..13 · confirmed · [Muret, 2020-10-10](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/761302555308720148/764590003887407154)
