The community findings behind [Bannerlord Materials, Textures and Shaders](../01-materials-textures-shaders.md), as mined from the Discord export, with a jump link to the message for each one.

- **Access:** links open in Discord and only work for members of the Mount & Blade Modding server. Deleted messages and departed users won't resolve.
- **Where links land:** each link points to the best-matching message by the credited author on that date. That is usually the statement itself, sometimes the question or reply next to it, so read the surrounding thread.
- **Paraphrase:** claim text is paraphrased. The credited people are the source; the wording is ours.

### MATERIALS (#bl-3d-art)

- **Standard shader is `pbr_metallic`** (Lambertian diffuse, GGX specular). Maps: Albedo, NormalMap, Metallic(R)+Gloss(G)+AO(B), optional tiling DetailNormalmap, Heightmap for POM/tessellation.<br>gkx (TW dev), 2020-04-28 · confirmed · [gkx, 2020-04-28](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/704643764055965707)
- **Packed "Specular Texture" layout** : Metallic = red, Gloss = green (inverted roughness), AO = blue, alpha = translucency (vegetation shaders only). The editor labels it Specular but it is not classic specular. Invert roughness to gloss before export.<br>gkx 2020-10-02, Fddd/npc99 2020-07-28/29, docs quoted 2020-10-11 · confirmed · [npc99., 2020-07-29](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/738052844770557995)
- **Flag fixes for new materials** : a newly created material lacks the `use_specular` flag (a tool bug), so the spec map does nothing until you tick it. For a heightmap to drive parallax you must tick `seperate_displacement_map`.<br>gkx, 2020-10-02 (PlebeianKilo also had to turn on specular to get shine, 2020-10-14) · confirmed · [gkx, 2020-10-02](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/761602773249687594)
- **Texture naming picks compression** : `_d` albedo, `_n` normal (auto BC5), `_s` metal/gloss/AO, `_h` height. If you don't follow it, normals default to DXT1 and look blocky. You can change compression in the texture editor (BC1/3/4/5/6/7). Textures still work with other names.<br>gkx (pinned) 2020-10-02/07, John_M 2020-10-15 · confirmed · [gkx, 2020-10-02](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/761606660225105951)
- **Compression choices that worked** : BC7 for colour/spec and BC5 for normals looked best. Vanilla "stacked" armour textures inspected in TpacTool used DXT1/DXT5, with BC5 for normals and albedos. Swyter's advice is BC3 over BC2, BC7 over BC3, and BC1 only for noisy environment textures.<br>Apalmemnom 2020-10-08, npc99 2020-07-30, Swyter (pinned) 2020-07-30 · single-report (Swyter's is opinion) · [npc99., 2020-07-30](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/738358775844438138)
- **A texture whose size isn't a multiple of 4 crashed the material editor** ; resizing fixed it.<br>Apalmemnom, 2020-10-17 · single-report · [Apalmemnom, 2020-10-17](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/767178395255570455)
- **Alpha-tested helmet parts cost the whole helmet** : with alpha on any part, the whole helmet is drawn without early depth/stencil test. TW splits fur or grille into a separate alpha material and keeps the rest opaque.<br>gkx, 2020-04-28 · confirmed (dev) · [gkx, 2020-04-28](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/704649487439757423)
- **Hair cards have no flow map** : strands are unwrapped all in one direction, and the dev admits the hair shader lacks features. Skin uses Kelemen/Szirmay-Kalos specular (Intensity R, Gloss G, AO B) with screen-space SSS. Foliage translucency is stored in the alpha of the M/G/AO texture.<br>gkx, 2020-04-28 · confirmed (dev) · [gkx, 2020-04-28](https://discord.com/channels/411286129317249035/697071240015380500/704643764055965707)
- **Faction/banner colouring** : vanilla `armour_roman_mail_shirt` uses the shader flag `use_double_colormap_with_mask_texture`. The diffuse2 texture's red and green channels mask where the primary and secondary faction colours apply.
